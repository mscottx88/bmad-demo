**[Metaphor #1]: The Wise Genie Lamp**
_Concept_: The whole demo is framed as a genie lamp. You rub the lamp and make a wish (your product idea). Rather than blindly granting it, the **wise** genie coaches the wish into shape with questions — which *is* the BMAD method (brief, PRD, clarifying Qs) reimagined as a magical conversation. Then: POOF, it grants the wish and builds it.
_Novelty_: Reframes BMAD's "annoying" upfront questioning as the genie making sure your wish comes out perfect — turning process into delight, not friction.

**[Payoff #2]: The Treasure Reveal (Pile of Cash)**
_Concept_: The POOF moment doesn't show abstract code — it rains down treasure: coins, plus a spinning reward counter ("⏱️ 6 hours saved," "📝 PRD written," "✅ 12 tests passing"). The demo rewards you for making a good wish.
_Novelty_: Leads with the *payoff/value* an engineer feels ("that just saved me an afternoon") instead of the mechanics. Gamifies adoption — using the method feels like winning, not homework.
